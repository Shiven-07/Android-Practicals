AIM: To develop an Android application that demonstrates all the Activity Lifecycle methods and
analyze their execution using Logcat.
THEORY
An Activity is a single screen in an Android application. Android manages the lifecycle of an activity
through a series of callback methods. These methods are automatically invoked depending on the
activity's state, such as launching, pausing, stopping, restarting, or destroying.
Android Activity Lifecycle Methods
    1.  onCreate()
         o  Called when the activity is created.
         o  Used for initialization and loading the UI.
    2.  onStart()
         o  Called when the activity becomes visible to the user.
    3. onResume()
         o  Called when the activity comes to the foreground and is ready for user interaction.
    4.  onPause()
         o  Called when another activity partially covers the current activity.
    5.  onStop()
         o  Called when the activity is no longer visible.
    6.  onRestart()
         o  Called after the activity has been stopped and before it starts again.
    7.  onDestroy()
         o  Called before the activity is destroyed.
